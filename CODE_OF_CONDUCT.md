# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Loops a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, but accept responsibility for impact.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Welcome people who are still learning.
- Disagree with ideas without attacking people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Sexualized attention or imagery.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or ban contributors who violate this code of conduct.

If you need to report a concern, contact the maintainers through the project's security or maintainer contact channel once one is published.
