## Project transfer decision

For each relevant idea choose one:

- `adopt`: use substantially as tested;
- `adapt`: change it for our setting and label it as an adaptation;
- `reproduce`: follow a bounded paper claim closely enough for a reproduction;
- `reject`: evidence or risk argues against using it;
- `defer`: potentially useful, but not justified yet.

Record the decision, reason, implementation surface, required experiment, and promotion gate.
